### What BHCs in the Philippines Officially Submit to the City Health Office (Use this as your checklist during your interview):

1. **M2 / FHSIS Monthly Consolidation Report**:
   - **Morbidity / Consultation Report**: Top diseases diagnosed (Dengue, Upper Respiratory Infections, Hypertension, TB, Diarrhea, etc.).
   - **National Immunization Program (NIP) / Child Vaccine Report**:
     - Number of Infants/Toddlers vaccinated by dose: BCG, HepB, Pentavalent (1st, 2nd, 3rd dose), OPV/IPV, and MMR/Measles.
     - Fully Immunized Children (FIC) count.
   - **Maternal Health & Family Planning**: Prenatal checkups, ultrasounds conducted.

2. **PIDSR (Philippine Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response)**:
   - Weekly / Monthly Notifiable Communicable Disease Reports:
     - **Dengue cases** (for outbreak and vector control).
     - **Tuberculosis (NTP / TB-DOTS)** cases and referrals.
     - **HIV/STI** referrals.
     - **Measles** cases.

3. **Medicine Inventory & Utilization Report (Logistics)**:
   - Monthly physical count of medicines.
   - Total units dispensed/claimed by residents vs. stock remaining.
   - Damaged, expired, or returned medicine quantity reports.

4. **BHW Accomplishment Report (BAR)**:
   - Total home visits / home services conducted.
   - Medicine deliveries completed (with claiming receipts and proof of delivery).
   - Walk-in patient registrations.

### How MedLink is already aligned with these reports:
- **Surveillance & Consultations Tab**: Covers **PIDSR / Morbidity** (Dengue, TB, HIV, Measles with threshold epidemic warnings and printable CHO summaries).
- **Child Vaccinations (Baby Book) Tab**: Covers **NIP / FHSIS Immunization Report** (1st dose, 2nd dose, 3rd dose, booster tracking).
- **Medicine Delivery & Claiming Receipts**: Covers **Medicine Utilization & BHW Accomplishment Report** (delivery proof, digital signatures, returned medicines).
- **Reports & Analytics (Export to Excel & Print)**: Covers **Monthly Service Counts & Resident Medicine Consumption**.
